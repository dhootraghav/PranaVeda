Nutrition & Diet

Vata Balance Warm Foods Hydration

Focus on grounding, cooked meals to balance excess Vata energy. Incorporate root vegetables, healthy fats like ghee, and warming spices such as ginger and cumin into your daily routine. Try to minimize cold or raw foods, especially in the late evening.

🌿

Lifestyle Optimisation

Dinacharya Sleep Hygiene Routine

Establish a consistent morning routine to anchor your nervous system. We suggest waking up around 6 AM, practicing daily tongue scraping, and dedicating 15 minutes to gentle, mindful stretching before looking at any screens or beginning your workday.

🧘

Mindfulness

Pranayama Nadi Shodhana Meditation

Integrate 10 minutes of Nadi Shodhana (alternate nostril breathing) right before bed. This specific breathwork will help clear mental clutter, calm your energetic pathways, and prepare your physical body for deep, uninterrupted and restorative rest.
